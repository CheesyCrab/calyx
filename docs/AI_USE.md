# AI Use

## Development assistance

Coding-agent and image-generation tools assisted the development of Calyx,
including implementation, review, documentation, and visual exploration.

## Human responsibility

Cheesy Crab remains accountable for the published work. Assisted material is
reviewed and must meet the same correctness, quality, and maintainability rules
as material created without those tools.

## Assets and licensing

Assisted assets follow the same provenance, licensing, and redistribution rules
as other assets. Asset-specific provenance is recorded locally when ordinary
source or license facts require it; Calyx does not maintain a separate
asset-by-asset AI ledger.

## Runtime behavior and privacy

Calyx performs no runtime generation, AI-service call, prompt transfer, or
player-data transfer. Playing a cart does not send prompts, cart state, or
player input to an AI service.

## Contributions

Contributors are responsible for understanding, reviewing, licensing, and
explaining assisted work. Substantial tool-generated content must be disclosed
in the contribution description and follows the same review and licensing
rules as every other contribution.
